# Floor data

Floor uses human-written buyer messages from CraigslistBargains, collected by the Stanford NLP Cocoa project. Source: [Cocoa](https://stanfordnlp.github.io/cocoa/) / [repository](https://github.com/stanfordnlp/cocoa); He, Chen, Balakrishnan and Liang (2018), [Decoupling Strategy and Generation in Negotiation Dialogues](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.09637). The Hugging Face dataset revision and raw-file hashes are recorded in `data/existing_source.json`.

Buyer utterances are copied from the corpus. Offer amounts are derived from source annotations and explicit amounts in the message. Floor's seller minimum and default full-payment/collection assumptions are synthetic task policy, not historical facts about the original negotiations. The derived training/validation split keeps dialogues separate.

Additional negotiation scenarios were generated by two gpt-6-luna subagents. They are marked `synthetic_gpt-6-luna`. Their wording and labels received a rapid correction pass; they are not conversations with actual Floor users. Replies used for supervised learning are rendered from a deterministic negotiation policy.

The model is Qwen/Qwen3-8B, fine-tuned and sampled through Tinker. The demo's seed inquiries and README screenshot are synthetic examples. The development comparisons do not establish real sales or a human usability study. The original human corpus is not redistributed in this repository; its dataset card marks its license as unknown.

The interface bundles Space Grotesk and DM Sans. Their SIL Open Font License files are included alongside the fonts in `static/fonts/`.
